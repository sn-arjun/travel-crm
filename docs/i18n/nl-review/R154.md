# R154 machine-made Dutch for review

Departure-relative messages (scheduled message rules, preview, template variables). Screen texts are in `desktop/src/messages/nl/departureMessages.json`; the starter rules, their email templates and the staff notification and suggestion texts are in `backend/src/i18n/nl.ts` (keys `departureMessages.*`).
