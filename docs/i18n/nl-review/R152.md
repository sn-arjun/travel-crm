# R152 Dutch texts to review

Machine-made Dutch for the margin-scheme screens (`margin.*`) in `desktop/src/messages/nl/margin.json`. Arabic is machine-made too (`ar/margin.json`). The legal wording presets themselves (`Bijzondere regeling reisbureaus` and the EU and UK mentions) are not translations: they are the mentions as the rules phrase them, and the agency chooses and edits them with its accountant.
