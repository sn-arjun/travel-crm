This policy covers the information we collect on this website. It does not cover the customer data that an agency stores inside its own TravelCRM Pro account; for that data the agency decides why and how it is used and we act as its processor (see the Data Processing Addendum).

2. What we collect on this website

  • Enquiry forms: your name, email, optional phone or WhatsApp number, agency name, city or country, team size, your message, the page you sent it from, and which kind of request you chose (demo, trial or question).
  • Technical data: a one-way scrambled form (salted hash) of your IP address, kept with the enquiry to spot abuse. We do not store the address itself with the enquiry. Our hosting provider may log requests as part of normal operation.
  • We do not run advertising trackers on this page. Owner: update this line if analytics or cookies are added.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your request and, if you ask for a trial, to set up your agency by hand.
  • To keep the website and form safe (rate limits, spam filtering, optional Cloudflare Turnstile check).
  • We do not sell your information and we do not use enquiries for advertising.

4. Who receives it

Our service providers receive it only to run the service: hosting and storage (Cloudflare), email delivery (SendGrid). The full list is on the sub-processors page.
