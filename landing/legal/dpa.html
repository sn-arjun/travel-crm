This addendum applies when [Legal entity name: owner to fill in landing/config.js] ("processor") processes personal data on behalf of an agency ("controller") through TravelCRM Pro. It forms part of the Terms of Service.

1. Subject matter and duration

We host and process the agency's customer, lead, traveller, quote, invoice, message and trip data so that the agency can use the service, for as long as the agreement lasts.

2. Instructions

We process personal data only on the agency's documented instructions, which are the agency's use of the service and its settings, unless the law requires otherwise.

3. Types of data and people

Names, phone numbers, email addresses, travel plans, passport and visa details entered by the agency, payment records and message content, relating to the agency's customers, travellers, suppliers and staff.

4. Confidentiality and security

Staff with access are bound to confidentiality. We apply access controls by role, encrypted connections, encryption of stored provider credentials and an activity log.
