How it works

From the first message to the hotel voucher

  1. A message arrives

    WhatsApp, SMS, email or your website form. It lands in one inbox, filed under the right customer.

  2. The assistant reads it

    Destination, dates, number of travellers and budget are picked out of the message, and it suggests creating the contact and lead.

  3. You build the quote

    Start from the closest itinerary template, with prices from your rate cards, and get a day-by-day plan with a branded PDF.

  4. You send it and follow up

    The reply is drafted for you to read and send. Once sent, the lead moves to Proposal Sent and a follow-up is scheduled.

  5. The customer says yes

    The invoice and the hotel vouchers are drafted for you. You approve each step.

The assistant

An assistant that suggests. You decide.

The assistant works in the background on your enquiries. It never sends a message to a customer on its own, and it never records money without asking.

Reads every message

Sorts new enquiries from payment news and questions, and prepares the right step.

Asks before it acts

Each automation is off, ask-first or automatic. Only an admin can switch one to automatic, and only low-risk steps that do not message a customer can be automatic. Customers who opted out are never drafted a message.

Everything is on record

Every step is logged, and most can be undone with one click. One switch pauses all automations.

AI reading is metered in monthly credits that come with your plan. When credits run out, the assistant keeps working on plain rules.
