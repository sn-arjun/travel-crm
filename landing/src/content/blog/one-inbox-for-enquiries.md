Most agencies do not lose customers on price. They lose them in the gap between a WhatsApp message at 10 pm and a reply the next afternoon, or when the one person who knew the customer is on leave.

## Put every channel in one place

Connect your WhatsApp Business number, your enquiry email and your website form. Each message lands in **one inbox**, threaded by customer, whichever channel it came from. A customer who wrote on WhatsApp and then emailed a passport copy has one thread, not two.

## Let the first step prepare itself

When a new enquiry arrives, the destination, dates, number of travellers and budget are picked out of the message, and a lead is suggested. You read it and click Apply. The first reply can be drafted for you to check and send.

## Assign it straight away

Leads can be assigned by weight, round-robin or source, so the right person sees it first. Response-time targets flag messages left without a reply.

## Follow up on a schedule

A follow-up sequence per status means nobody has to remember. "Quote sent, no reply in two days" becomes a reminder with a drafted message, waiting for a person to send it.

## Measure what matters

The dashboard shows enquiries by source, how fast they were answered and how many became bookings. Each number opens the list behind it.
